About me

A little more about who's behind the camera.

photo — placeholder

Hi, I'm Tracy — you might know me as Kata.

I'm a Canadian creator with Dominican roots, splitting my content between two cultures and two languages. My audience is genuinely mixed — English and Spanish-speaking followers who come for the same reason: honest, easy-to-trust recommendations instead of a hard sell.

I started out unboxing and reviewing PR packages on TikTok, and that's grown into full UGC production — lifestyle content, travel diaries, and product features designed to feel like a recommendation from a friend, not an ad.

When I'm not filming, I'm usually planning the next trip. Travel and adventure content is where I feel most myself, and it's a big part of how I show my followers the products and experiences that actually make their day-to-day better.
